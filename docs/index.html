Don't Let Me Slouch

Gentle posture nudges — 100% on your Mac.

What it does

Don't Let Me Slouch is a calm menu-bar coach. Sit up straight, calibrate once, and it gently nudges you when you start to slump — with a sound, a soft screen-edge glow, or an on-screen banner. Everything runs on your Mac; your camera feed is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Requires macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, with a camera.

Common tips: grant camera access in System Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Camera, keep the window open (or use “Compact window”) while monitoring, and re-Calibrate after you change seats or cameras.

Privacy

No data is collected. No accounts, no analytics, no network — all posture detection happens on-device with Apple Vision.
