Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Data collected: none

Don't Let Me Slouch is designed to be completely private. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Camera

Don't Let Me Slouch uses your Mac’s camera only to detect your sitting posture, in real time, on your device, using Apple’s built-in Vision framework.

  • Your camera feed is never recorded to disk.
  • It is never uploaded or sent over any network.
  • No images or video ever leave your Mac.
  • The app makes no network connections and has no servers.

Accounts & tracking

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing about your usage is tracked.

Settings

Your preferences (sensitivity, alert style, etc.) are stored locally on your Mac in standard app settings and never leave the device.

Children

Don't Let Me Slouch does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? See the support page.
